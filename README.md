# Word Pronunciation Notebook

A small page for studying English words and expressions.

- Search a word to see real pronunciation clips (YouGlish), example sentences and the dictionary definition.
- Write three practice sentences per word.
- Past words are kept in the History view.

Your words stay on your own computer: in the browser, and optionally in a JSON file you choose (Chrome/Edge). Use Export/Import to back up or move them.

Videos come from YouTube via YouGlish. See the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
