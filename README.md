# Word Pronunciation Notebook

A small page for studying English words and expressions.

- Search a word to see real pronunciation clips (YouGlish), the dictionary definition and example sentences.
- Listen to the word, record yourself, and keep a short Korean memo per word.
- Write three practice sentences per word and check them for spelling and basic grammar.
- Past words are kept in the History view as new, practicing or learned.
- Copy the last 7 days of sentences to send to a tutor.

Your words stay in your own browser. Use Export/Import at the bottom of the History view to back up or move them.

When you press the sentence check, your sentences are sent to [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org/).
Videos come from YouTube via YouGlish. See the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
